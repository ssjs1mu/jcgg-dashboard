ItemDetail

3. Heat-map and trading call

ItemDetail
Popup chart with levelsCandles (5/15 min) with EMA9, EMA21 and VWAP, plus colour-coded level lines: red = stop-loss, cyan = targets, magenta = OI call wall, lime = OI put wall, gold = most-active strike, amber = day high/low, grey = previous day high/low, light red / green = pivot resistances / supports. Tick boxes for Pivots, OI, Fib. Button "TradingView live chart" opens the full chart in a new tab; "Copy levels for TradingView" copies a Pine script that draws the same lines on it (TradingView › Pine Editor › paste › Add to chart). TradingView cannot take drawn levels through a link, hence the script.
Option OI table (popup)Top-3 call/put OI strikes, most-active strikes, stop-loss walls, broken/sustain status
UniverseNifty 100 + MidSmallCap 400 = 499 stocks (about 495-497 return data)
Filters (all on by default)Open = Low (within 0.05%), above previous close, volume at least 1.5x average (1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 selectable), pace-adjusted for the share of the session elapsed. Group: All / Large 100 / Mid+Small 399. Show 36 / 60 / 120 / All
OrderingStocks matching every filter float to the top, then by how many filters match, then by % move
Cell contentSymbol, % move, volume multiple, ▲ profit to target 1, ▼ loss to stop-loss
Trading callEntry = last price. Side by vote of EMA 9/21, EMA 10/20, EMA 50, VWAP, standard pivot, live pivot (50% or more bullish = BUY; 70% or more / 30% or less = strong). Stop-loss = EMA 50 if within 3%, else nearest support. Targets 1 and 2 = nearest levels beyond 0.25% of price. Shows profit, loss and reward:risk
Stocks without intraday barsOnly the top 40 by filter rank have 5-min bars; the rest use a day-range call (day high/low, live pivot, previous close)
Blinking ★ (top 3)Must match all filters, be a BUY with profit to target 1 of at least 0.5% and loss of at most 3%, have intraday bars, volume of the latest bars at least 10% above the bars before (rising), price above VWAP and EMA9 above EMA21. Ranked by reward:risk. Soft 2.6 s pulse
🐟 BIG FISH triggerViolet glow cell with a fish mark, floated to the top. A stock is a Big Fish only when ALL hold: price at least Rs 50; day volume above 3.5 lakh; relative volume (pace-adjusted) at least 1.5x; day range (high-low) at least 1.5%; price at least 2% above today's open; RSI 14 (daily, with today's price) above 55; price above the previous 1-month (20-session) high. Same violet colour and fish mark in Live alerts
🔻 Bear sideOrder: Big Fish Short tiles pinned first (rare, time-critical); then all flagged tiles (Big Fish, matching bull, bear) with the 3 priority stars leading, bull and bear mixed by technical strength = (day range % + move %) x volume pace (capped at 5) x technical factor (1.3 when the intraday call is strong and agrees with the move's direction, 1.1 agrees, 0.9 disagrees), Big Fish x1.6. Bear tiles also include strong SELL calls down 2% or more on 1.5x volume pace, so positions change as volatility and volume change. Bear tile (🔻, rose glow): Open = High (within 0.05%), price below previous close, volume at least the chosen multiple of average. red fish + 🔻 BIG FISH SHORT (orange-red glow, pinned at the very top): price at least Rs 50; day volume above 3.5 lakh; relative volume BELOW 1.2x; day range at least 1.5%; price at least 1.5% below today's open; RSI 14 below 40; price below the previous 1-month low. Same colours in Live alerts. A colour legend sits under the heat-map header
Click a cellOpens intraday technicals popup: timeframe 5 or 15 min; EMA 9/21 and 10/20 with above/below; EMA 50 as the day's stop-loss; VWAP; standard pivots; live pivots; Fibonacci 23.6 / 38.2 / 50 / 61.8 / 78.6% from the day high; volume trend vs previous bars and vs yesterday; verdict; colour-coded level ladder with the CMP marked; the trading call
DataBranch heat (heat.json, bars.json) rebuilt every 5 min in market hours; live quotes through Apps Script when available; page refresh 45 s open / 180 s closed

7. Market barometer and Fear & Greed

ItemDetail
TilesAdvancing / Declining, Average move, Day highs / lows (within 0.2% of the day extreme), 1-month highs / lows. 52-week counts are in the Fear & Greed tooltip
Fear & Greed score (0-100)Average of: India VIX (25 = 0 to 10 = 100), % of stocks advancing, share of 1-month highs among highs + lows, Nifty day move (-2% = 0, +2% = 100). Zones: below 25 extreme fear, below 45 fear, below 55 neutral, below 75 greed, else extreme greed. This is our own score, not an official index
Market stance iconContrarian reading of the Fear & Greed score (more greed = more risk, more fear = more opportunity): 75+ 🔥 overheated - avoid chasing; 55-75 ⚠️ getting risky - tighten stops; 45-55 ⚖️ balanced - stock-specific; 25-45 🛒 opportunity building - stagger buys; below 25 💎 opportunity - accumulate in stages. If the score is under 45 and 2 or more cross-asset factors are adverse, the line adds: wait, macro adverse. A general reading, not a trade signal
DataUses the heat-map feed already loaded; re-evaluated after every heat-map load and every 15 s

8. Early reversal alerts

CheckRule
Index turn (Nifty, Bank Nifty, Sensex)UP: price at least 0.25% above the day low, the low made within the last 20 readings, last 4 readings rising, the 8 before the low falling. DOWN: mirror image from the day high. EARLY from 0.25%, CONFIRMED above 0.6%
Top-40 stocks15-min EMA9 crosses EMA21 within the last 3 bars, with the latest 2 bars' volume above the 2 before
Sudden cross-asset moves (30 min)RISK-OFF when India VIX rises 3%, USDINR rises 0.15%, Brent rises 1.2%, US $ index rises 0.25%, US 10Y yield rises 2%, or Dow futures fall 0.4%. RISK-ON for the opposite moves. These limits are my starting values and can be changed
TilesOne tile per factor with its 30-min move (pink adverse, green supportive, cyan below limit; adverse tiles pulse softly) and an adverse / supportive count
NoteLive feed readings are 15-min apart, snapshot readings 5-min apart; the 30-min window adjusts to the source
