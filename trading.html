EMA = exponential moving average on closing prices of the chosen timeframe (warmed up over the last 5 sessions) · VWAP = volume-weighted average price of today's session · Standard pivot from the previous session · Live pivot and Fibonacci retracement from today's high/low · EMA50 used as the day's stop-loss reference.
