⚙ Brokerage, charges & interest (per client)
Uploaded trade books and ledgers stay in this browser only - nothing is sent anywhere or saved online. Intraday = the part of a client's buys and sells in the same symbol on the same day that offsets; the rest is delivery. Net rate includes brokerage and all charges (buy: rate + charges, sell: rate − charges). Holding cost = interest on the net cost of each delivery lot from T+1 to today (open) or to the sale date (closed). Prices: live during market hours, last close otherwise.
