How an entry is judged. Only what was known before the entry day is used (previous completed bar, the 20-day range, the fill price itself). 11 rules, ✔ = passed, ✖ = failed, – = not enough data. Critical rules (trend above the 50-DMA, not chasing a gap or spike, not at the range top without a breakout, 2%+ room to the next resistance, not a relief bounce) — one critical failure = RISKY; the trend rule failing, or two critical failures = WRONG; otherwise RIGHT. Stop and targets shown for each entry are what the plan would have been: stop = just under the nearest support (20 / 50-DMA, 10-day low, last week's low, daily S1) 1.5–4% below the fill, else a 4% cap; T1 = 1.5R, T2 = 3R. The trade book carries no client names or account codes (letters A–G are arbitrary tags). Daily prices are the free Yahoo feed through the app's data backend; rule-based review, not advice.
