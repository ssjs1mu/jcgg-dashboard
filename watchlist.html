How the call is made. On the chosen timeframe: EMA 9 vs EMA 21 and a fresh cross, the slope of EMA 21, price vs EMA 9, price vs today's VWAP (Daily: vs 20-day average), RSI 14, and whether volume is rising with the move. Their agreement gives a score from -100 to +100; BUY at +45 or more, SELL at -45 or less, otherwise AVOID. Conviction is the size of that score. Entry is the current price; the exit (stop-loss) sits beyond EMA 21 and at least one ATR away; T1/T2/T3 are 1, 2 and 3 times that risk. Prices and triggers update every 10 seconds in market hours (bars and EMAs every 30 seconds) (every 5 minutes otherwise) and the table re-sorts itself by conviction.
