Choose a symbol and exchange, then press Calculate.
How the levels are built: Daily pivots use the previous completed session, Weekly the previous completed week, Monthly the previous completed month (high, low, close; DeMark also uses that period's open). Classic P = (H+L+C)/3 · Fibonacci = P ± 0.382 / 0.618 / 1.000 × range · Camarilla = C ± range × 1.1/12, /6, /4, /2 · Woodie P = (H+L+2C)/4 · DeMark from the period's open/close relation · CPR = P with BC = (H+L)/2 and TC = 2P − BC. Support or resistance is decided by where the level sits against the live price: every level above CMP is resistance (pink), every level below is support (green) — whatever its name. A resistance the price has crossed turns into support (↺); the nearest crossed one is marked trail SL. When the price is beyond R3 / S3 the extension levels (R4, R5 / S4, S5, marked ext) are added so there is always resistance above and support below. Trend arrow: ▲ Bullish when the live price is above the pivot (above R1 = strong), ▼ Bearish below it (below S1 = strong), ◆ Neutral inside the pivot zone; Camarilla reads above R3 / below S3 as breakout / breakdown, inside as range-bound; CPR reads above TC / below BC. Major levels = price zones where several pivot levels, the previous day / week / month high-low, the 20-day and 52-week high-low and the 20 / 50 / 200-day averages fall together (within ~0.3%); more levels and longer time frames = stronger. Rule-based levels, not advice.
